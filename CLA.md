"Submission Date" means the date on which You Submit a Contribution to Us.

whichever is earlier.

## Annex B: How to agree

You agree to this Agreement by adding one line for Yourself to the table in `CLA-SIGNATURES.md` in the dictate
repository, in a commit whose author is You, as part of Your first Submission (pull request or merge request). The line
names You, the account You Submit from, the date and the version of this Agreement. By adding it, You agree to that
version of this Agreement, as it reads in `CLA.md` in the same commit, for all Your Contributions to dictate from the
Effective Date on. A later version of this Agreement applies to You only if You agree to it in the same way.

We accept the Agreement by merging that commit. There is no separate signature by Us; the repository history is the
record of both.
